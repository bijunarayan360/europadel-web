Cancellation policy.

Courts are in demand. Letting us know early means someone else gets to play. Draft · to be confirmed

Court bookings

Court booking cancellation rules
When you cancelWhat happens
More than 24 hours beforeCancel free of charge. Online payments are refunded in full to the original card or Apple Pay. Pay-at-club bookings owe nothing. A member's free session returns to their allowance.
Less than 24 hours beforeThe booking is charged in full and cannot be refunded. If you need to move it, message reception on WhatsApp and we will try to find another time.
No-showIf you have not checked in 15 minutes after your start time, the booking is marked as a no-show and the court is released. Online payments for no-shows are not refunded, and repeated no-shows may mean future bookings must be paid online in advance.
Late arrivalYour session still ends at the booked time, so the next players can start on time.
If the club cancelsIf we have to close a court for maintenance or an event, you choose a full refund or a free move to another time.

Coaching sessions

  • Cancel or move a coaching session free of charge up to 24 hours before it starts.
  • Within 24 hours, or if you don't show, the session is charged in full. A session from a 5 or 10 package is used up.
  • If your coach is unavailable, we will offer another coach, another time or a full refund.

Equipment hire

  • Racket, paddle and ball hire is refunded with the booking it was added to.
  • Hire equipment is returned to reception at the end of your session. Lost or damaged items may be charged.

How to cancel

  • Members: sign in, open My Account and choose Cancel next to the booking.
  • Guests: message reception on WhatsApp at +971 52 539 2908 with your booking reference.
  • Refunds to cards and Apple Pay usually reach your account within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank.

Memberships

  • Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice.
  • Annual plans run for the full twelve months and can be paused within your plan's allowance.
