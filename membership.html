Questions

Good to know.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes. Annual Standard members can pause for up to 2 weeks a year, and annual Platinum and Business Club members for up to 4 weeks. Ask at reception or message us on WhatsApp.

How do free court bookings work?

Sign in and book as normal. When your booking falls within your plan, the court is free. Every plan includes one free 90-minute session per day, on padel or pickleball.

Can I bring guests?

Of course. Your guests simply pay their share of the walk-in court price. Members of every tier can book for a full group of four.

How do I pay?

Online with Apple Pay or card, or at the club by cash or card. Your membership starts as soon as your first payment is confirmed.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. Annual plans run for the full twelve months.
