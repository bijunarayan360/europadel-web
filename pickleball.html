The game

Everyone plays.
Everyone laughs.

Pickleball mixes the best of tennis, badminton and table tennis on a smaller court with a solid paddle and a light, holed ball. It is gentle on the body, quick to pick up and properly competitive once you get going. Grandparents, teenagers and first-timers can all share a court.

How to play

Four rules, one great game.

Learn these and you are ready to play. The rest comes with your first few games.

Serve underhand

Serve diagonally, below the waist, from behind the baseline. Only the serving side can win a point.

Let it bounce twice

The return of serve and the next shot must each bounce once before anyone volleys. It keeps rallies going.

Stay out of the kitchen

The zone either side of the net is the kitchen. You can step in, but you cannot volley from inside it.

Games to 11

First to 11 points, winning by two. Games are quick, so there is always time for one more.
