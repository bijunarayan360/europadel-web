Europadel · Website bookings

Live booking setup guide.

How to switch the website from preview to live bookings. Every court booking, coaching session, membership and interest form is recorded in the club's Google Sheet, with live court availability and card or Apple Pay payments through Stripe.

How it works.

1 · Customer booksPicks court, time and equipment on the website.
2 · Court is heldThe sheet is checked live. Taken slots can't be booked.
3 · PaymentCard or Apple Pay on Stripe, or pay at the club.
4 · RecordedA row appears in the sheet. Reception gets an email.

Card payments hold the court for 30 minutes while the customer pays. If they don't finish, the court is released automatically. A checker runs every 5 minutes in the background and confirms any payment that arrived late.

Setup, step by step.

  1. Club · 2 minutes

    Create the Google Sheet

    • Sign in to the club's Google account and open sheets.google.com.
    • Click Blank spreadsheet and name it Europadel Bookings.
    • Share it with reception and management as Editors.
  2. Developer · 5 minutes

    Add the booking script

    • In the sheet, open Extensions → Apps Script.
    • Delete the sample code, paste the whole of Code.gs, and click Save.
  3. Developer · 3 minutes

    Add the settings

    In Apps Script, open Project Settings (the gear icon) → Script properties → Add script property:

    PropertyValue
    SITE_URLhttps://europadel.ae (no slash at the end)
    STRIPE_SECRET_KEYFrom Stripe → Developers → API keys. Use sk_test_… for testing, then sk_live_…. Leave it out and the site offers pay at club only.
    NOTIFY_EMAILReception's email. Gets a message for every booking and new member.
    CUSTOMER_EMAILSyes to email customers a confirmation (see email limits below).
    ADMIN_KEYThe staff password for the dashboard (europadel.ae/admin.html). Use a long password and share it only with staff.
    AUTO_RELEASE_UNPAIDyes to cancel unpaid pay-at-club bookings 15 minutes before start. Leave no until reception is used to marking "Paid at club".
  4. Developer · 2 minutes

    Run setup once

    • In the toolbar, choose the function setup and click Run.
    • Google asks for permission. Choose the club account, then Advanced → Go to project (unsafe) → Allow. This warning appears for every private script; it's expected.
    • The sheet now has the tabs Today, Bookings, Members, Blocks, Interest, and the automatic checkers are switched on.
  5. Developer · 2 minutes

    Publish the script as a web app

    • Deploy → New deployment → type Web app.
    • Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone.
    • Click Deploy and copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec).

    After any later change to the code, use Deploy → Manage deployments → Edit → Version: New version. The URL stays the same.

  6. Developer · 1 minute

    Connect the website

    Open site/config.js and paste the URL:

    window.EP_CONFIG = {
  api: 'https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfy...../exec'
};

    Upload the site folder to the hosting. The "Preview" bar disappears and bookings go live.

  7. Club · 10 minutes

    Switch on Apple Pay in Stripe

    • Stripe Dashboard → Settings → Payment methods: turn on Cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
    • Under Settings → Branding, add the Europadel logo and olive colour (#4B5733) so the payment page matches the site.
  8. Everyone · 15 minutes

    Test before going live

    With the sk_test_ key, pay with Stripe's test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future expiry and any 3-digit code. Then work through the checklist below. When it all passes, swap in the sk_live_ key.

Test checklist.

  • Book a padel court by card. A row appears in Bookings as Confirmed · Card / Apple Pay (paid).
  • Try to book the same court and time again. It's crossed out on the website.
  • Start a card payment and press back on the Stripe page. The row shows Expired and the court is free again.
  • Book with "Pay at the club". The row shows Confirmed · Pay at club.
  • Add equipment. It's listed in the Equipment column and included in the amount.
  • Join a membership by card. A row appears in Members as Active with a member number.
  • Book as that member at a time the plan covers. The amount is AED 0 and Payment is Member.
  • Send the Register My Interest form. A row appears in Interest.
  • Reception receives the email notifications.

The staff dashboard.

Reception and management open europadel.ae/admin.html on any computer, tablet or phone, then enter the staff password (ADMIN_KEY). It isn't linked anywhere on the public site, and it reads and writes the same Google Sheet, so both always agree.

SectionWhat staff can do
Day summaryBookings, court occupancy, money paid, money still due at the club, and the rackets, paddles and balls to prepare.
Court gridEvery court from 6 AM to midnight, coloured by payment: paid, due at club, member, paying now, blocked. Coaching sessions have a gold edge. A red line marks the current time.
Manage a bookingClick it to see the customer and WhatsApp number, then mark paid at club, mark a no-show, cancel or restore.
Phone bookingsClick an empty space on the grid, or New booking. A double booking is refused.
Block a courtClose one court or all courts for maintenance, events or private hire. The website stops offering those times straight away.
MembersEvery member with plan, renewal date and status. Change the status (activate, pause, cancel) from the list.
InterestEvery Register My Interest form, with a WhatsApp link to reply.

The dashboard refreshes every minute on its own. Card refunds are still made in the Stripe dashboard.

For reception.

Everything below can also be done directly in the Google Sheet, which is useful if the dashboard isn't to hand.

TaskHow
See todayOpen the Today tab. It lists today's bookings by sport, court and time, and updates by itself.
Phone or walk-in bookingAdd a row in Bookings: Date 2026-10-12, Sport Padel, Activity Court, Start 18:00, End 19:30, Minutes 90, Court 2, name and WhatsApp, Status Confirmed, Source Phone. The website blocks that slot straight away.
Customer pays at the deskChange Payment to Paid at club.
Cancel a bookingSet Status to Cancelled. The slot opens on the website. If they paid by card and cancelled more than 24 hours ahead, refund in Stripe → Payments → the payment → Refund, then set Payment to Refunded.
No-showSet Status to No-show.
Close a courtAdd a row in Blocks: Date, Sport (Padel, Pickleball or All), Court (number or All), Start, End, Reason. Leave Start and End empty to close the whole day.
Member pays at the deskIn Members, change Status from Pay at club to Active and Payment to Paid at club.
Pause or cancel a memberSet Status to Paused or Cancelled. For monthly card members, also pause or cancel the subscription in Stripe → Customers.

Keep dates and times in the format shown (2026-10-12 and 18:00). Don't delete or rename columns or tabs; the website reads them by name.

Limits and next steps.

  • Email limits: Google limits how many emails a script can send each day: about 100 recipients a day on a free Gmail account and about 1,500 on Google Workspace. At 200–300 bookings a day, use Workspace, or set CUSTOMER_EMAILS to no and rely on the on-screen confirmation and Stripe's receipts.
  • Member sign-in: member benefits are matched by email address and noted "check member card" in the sheet. Reception should glance at the card at check-in. Verified Google sign-in can be added as a next step.
  • My Account: shows bookings made on that device. Customers message reception on WhatsApp for changes; reception updates the sheet.
  • Monthly memberships: renew automatically through Stripe. A daily check marks failed payments as Overdue, which stops free bookings until paid.
  • Later: if the club moves to dedicated booking software, the website design stays and the Stripe account can be kept.
