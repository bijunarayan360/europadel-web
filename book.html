Booking questions

Before you play.

Questions about a booking? Message reception on WhatsApp at +971 52 539 2908.

How many players can I bring?

Padel is played in doubles, so a padel court is for up to four players. Pickleball courts take singles or doubles, also up to four players. The court price covers everyone; split it however you like.

Do I need an account to book?

No. You can book as a guest with your full name, WhatsApp number and email. Create an account to see your bookings, coaching sessions and payment history in one place.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your start time. Members cancel in My Account; guests message reception on WhatsApp with their booking reference. Within 24 hours the booking is charged in full. Read the full policy →

What if I don't have a racket or paddle?

Add racket or paddle hire and balls in step 5 of your booking. Everything is ready to collect at reception when you arrive.

How do I pay?

Pay online with Apple Pay or a credit or debit card, or choose to pay at the club by cash or card. Pay-at-club bookings are held until 15 minutes after the start time. If you have not checked in and paid by then, the booking is cancelled and the court is released.

When should I arrive?

Arrive 10 to 15 minutes early to check in, collect any hire equipment and warm up. Sessions start and finish on the hour or half hour, so the next players can start on time.

Book padel and pickleball courts in Dubai Investment Park

Europadel is an indoor padel and pickleball club in Dubai Investment Park 1, with four padel courts and five pickleball courts in a 50,000 sq ft indoor building with 9-metre ceilings, fully air-conditioned with fresh-air FAHU units and 52 parking spaces on the plot. Book a court online for 60, 90 or 120 minutes, any day from 6 AM to midnight, with off-peak rates on weekday mornings and afternoons.

The club is about 10 minutes from Green Community, Expo City Dubai, Dubai South and Al Furjan, and 15 to 20 minutes from Jumeirah Golf Estates, Motor City, JVC, JLT and Dubai Marina. Book private, duo or group coaching for padel or pickleball, hire a racket or paddle, and stay for coffee in the mezzanine café overlooking the courts.
