Fresh air technology

Fresh air, every rally.

Europadel runs on the latest FAHU technology, a Fresh Air Handling Unit system built for large sports halls.

Not recirculated

Standard air-conditioning moves the same air around the room. Our FAHU units draw in fresh outside air instead.

Filtered, cooled, humidified

Every breath of air is filtered, cooled and humidified before it is blown into the courts.

Year-round play

Indoor and fully air-conditioned under 9-metre ceilings. August plays like January.

Club etiquette

A few small things that matter.

Simple habits keep the club running smoothly and make every game better for everyone.

Arrive early
Check in 10 to 15 minutes before your booking so you can start on time.
Court shoes only
Please wear clean padel, tennis or indoor court shoes. Running shoes can damage the turf.
Finish on time
Leave the court promptly when your time ends, so the next players can start theirs.
Respect the glass
Play the walls, never hit them in frustration. Keep rackets and paddles off the glass and net.
Mind the next court
Wait for a break in play before walking behind or collecting a ball from another court.
Leave it tidy
Take bottles, cans and towels with you, and return hire equipment to reception.
